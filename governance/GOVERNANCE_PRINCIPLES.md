# Governance principles — reference and clean-build
Approved manufacturer sources only; preserve chunk/page/provenance; vector store is rebuildable. Freeze corpora, evaluations and gold before qualification. Invalid evaluations remain invalid. Do not repair gold after exposure. Scope release claims to identical source, runtime, corpus/index, configuration, prompt, model, dataset, evaluator and run. Separate research/shadow from serving; explicitly approve any promotion. Fail visibly; provider fault and missing evidence are different. No secrets, customer data or internal credentials in this public repository. Avoid brand hardcoding and unchecked agent autonomy. Complexity needs downstream gain.
