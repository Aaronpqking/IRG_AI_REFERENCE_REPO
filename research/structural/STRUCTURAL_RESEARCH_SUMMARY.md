Review [late-run provenance](../../reports/reference/audits/LATE_RUN_PROVENANCE_RECONCILIATION_20261120.md) before treating any October/November figure as independently replayable.

An **exact source transfer proves code existed in the observed archive**, with recorded bytes/hash. It does **not** prove its activation in production, concurrent integration with other historical modules, test execution, empirical benefit, or a bound Git revision. A **curated report** provides locally intelligible observations but is not the original run ledger. `HISTORY_REPORTED_UNVERIFIED` figures below retain that separate authority label even when the mechanism is classified `PARTIAL_GAIN`, `NO_GAIN`, or `REGRESSION`. Those classifications are *scoped interpretations of available reports*, not release authorization.

### Evidence disposition vocabulary

`PROVEN`: specific locally supported fact or historically bound mechanism, within stated scope; `PROVEN_WITH_GAP`: implemented/source-backed mechanism lacking one or more empirical/runtime authority proofs; `PARTIAL_GAIN`: bounded measured stage gain with remaining failure(s); `NO_GAIN`: no measured benefit for the tested downstream outcome; `REGRESSION`: measured deterioration or unsafe tradeoff; `INVALID`: experiment validity failed; `BLOCKED`: missing required evidence/authority; `SUPERSEDED`: later scoped record displaces earlier interpretation, without altering original; `RESEARCH_ONLY`: code or diagnostic research not shown active in production; `UNPROVEN`: proposed effect lacks accepted evidence.

## Historical trajectory: runtime vs research vs evaluation

1. **V1 / V1.5 deployed-style foundation (July–August 2026):** approved PDFs, chunks and Qdrant; exact identifier and product DB discovery separate from semantic matching; lexical/metadata/dense PDF channels with hybrid fusion; document/configuration applicability; bounded neighbor expansion, one **45,000-character** shared model context and source projection. V1.5 was an **internal pilot candidate**, production readiness **not claimed**. Exact matching belongs to product identity and lexical authority, not a generic dense-similarity score. See [historical baseline](../../architecture/historical/V1_5_IMPLEMENTED_ARCHITECTURE_BASELINE.md), [hybrid retrieval source](../../source_reference/retrieval/hybrid_retrieval.py), [multi-source path](../../source_reference/retrieval/multi_source_retrieval.py), [unified context](../../source_reference/context/unified_answer_context.py). This is not evidence that later Docling and cross-encoder research ran in that same runtime.
2. **Structural loss boundary and shadow extraction (August–September):** August Gate 3 traced structural loss to converting parser-native PDF page objects into flat `page.get_text()`, losing geometry, vectors, imagery, table topology and diagram connectivity. The bounded 48-case / 14-page / 12-PDF audit reported **32 recovered visually verified relationships**, no lost controls and no newly false relationships with frozen annotations; three diagram-page text recoveries did not reconstruct graphic edges; one raster page remained OCR-dependent. The typed scorer on **16 valid/corrupt pairs** reported 100% precision/recall, while the term-presence scorer accepted 12 corrupted fixtures. **Recoverability and evaluator discrimination are not automated extractor qualification.** [August audit](../../reports/reference/structural/STRUCTURAL_GATE3_LOSS_BOUNDARIES_20260813.md).
3. **Docling shadow, page/owner/configuration/procedure (through September 9):** Docling, layout-region OCR, tables/header paths, page provenance, owner/model identity, procedure steps and warnings were investigated in quarantined research. The original [graph model](../../source_reference/structural/model.py) defines DOCUMENT/PAGE/SECTION/PARAGRAPH/TABLE/ROW/CELL/PROCEDURE/STEP/FIGURE/CAPTION/OCR_REGION nodes and parent-child relationships; [expansion](../../source_reference/structural/expansion.py) recovers related nodes, [owner identity](../../source_reference/structural/owner_identity.py) resolves named product/model ownership, and [configuration authority](../../source_reference/structural/configuration_authority_v1_3.py) distinguishes exact, compatible, unknown and conflicting configurations. [Procedure retriever V1.4](../../source_reference/structural/procedure_retriever_v1_4.py) builds scoped procedure views, orders steps and applies owner/intention checks. On a fresh **30-case** holdout, reported complete evidence **46.67% → 66.67%**, complete procedure evidence **16.67% → 75.00%**, procedure MRR **19.44% → 81.39%**. Yet cross-procedure contamination was **25.00%** and there were **3 new procedure failures**: `PARTIAL_GAIN`, **no promotion authorized**. The supplied local report is `CURATED_NOT_EXACT_TRANSFER`, and its original holdout ledger is absent.
4. **Canonical intermediate representation and TABLE research (September onward):** [Canonical IR V1.9.6](../../source_reference/structural/canonical_ir_v1_9_6.py) represents provenance-bearing documents, identity/applicability, relationships, sections, procedures, warnings, table rows/facts and figures, with source metadata vs filename/path-derived provenance distinguished. [V1.9.9b TABLE relation resolver](../../source_reference/structural/table_relation_resolver_v1_9_9b.py) defines table-only query/candidate relation semantics and explicit `MATCH`/`CONFLICT`/`UNKNOWN`; it leaves retrieval, authority and non-table selection outside its boundary. [V1.9.20 answerability detector](../../source_reference/structural/table_answerability_detector_v1_9_20.py) is **observational only**, with `UNIQUE`/`DUPLICATE_EQUIVALENT`/`MULTIPLE_DISTINCT`/`INSUFFICIENT`; it does not retrieve, rank, select or generate. Source implementation is `PROVEN_WITH_GAP`; no overall qualification gain follows from its existence.
5. **Canonical multiview, RRF and reranking research:** Derived sentence/paragraph/step/parent/table representations were made retrieval views of canonical evidence to separate retrieval surfaces from source authority. The [late canonical direct-RAG candidate V1.9.40](../../source_reference/structural/canonical_direct_rag_v1_9_40.py) explicitly describes **additive multi-domain retrieval over frozen V1.9.39** while retaining canonical IR, view records, vectors, RRF and citation pipeline; it does **not** establish promotion to the V1.5 production baseline. Local neural/cross-encoder research and product-aware technical reranking must be classified `RESEARCH_ONLY` until paired downstream evidence and actual serving activation are bound. Canonical collapse prevents multiple search representations being counted as independent facts.
6. **Invalid and adverse response-control experiments (September 24):** The locally retained [V1.9.33 report](../../reports/reference/ablations/V1_9_33_INVALID_ABLATION_REPORT.md) records a 110-case planned experiment stopped at case 21 by retrieval-parity failure; **20 completed observations do not qualify an aggregate**, classification `INVALID`. The [V1.9.35 report](../../reports/reference/ablations/V1_9_35_SINGLE_RETRIEVAL_FORKED_PATH_ABLATION_REPORT.md) records a **110/110 development-only paired replay** sharing one retrieval execution per case: response path A **82/110 correct**, path B **10/110**, with B preventing 20 unsupported cases while suppressing 82 correct ones. Exact-gold@10 on its distinct denominator **100/100** coexisted with **0/100 semantic answer-set reachability@10**. These metrics expose the cost of response-policy overcontrol, not a winning retrieval architecture or final qualified accuracy.
7. **October unseen P2, scale, November R2:** the unseen paired standard/full result, expanded scale and R2 authority/sufficiency traces below are `HISTORY_REPORTED_UNVERIFIED`. They establish decisive *provisional constraints* for the clean build, not reproducible release evidence.

### Explicit paired full-view accounting

The [local paired report](../../reports/reference/ablations/STANDARD_VS_FULL_VIEW_P2_ABLATION.md) and [current state](../../CURRENT_REFERENCE_STATE.md) record **20 planned / 19 executed / 17 valid unseen**. **P2-IND-05** lacked approved cleaning evidence and was blocked; **P2-IND-01** had conflicting frozen grounding and was excluded without repairing gold; a historical-fact control was separate. Both arms delivered **3/17** sufficient context, **5/17** sufficient evidence@10, and **zero critical wrong-product errors**. There were **zero full-view wins, zero losses, three shared successes and fourteen shared failures**. Of **18 valid ranking comparisons** reported, lexical/dense order changed in **17**, RRF/fusion in **9**, reranking in **7**; delivered context/evidence changed in one case and did not rescue it. These are **different denominators for ranking comparison versus valid unseen outcome**, and none is a generalization proof beyond this frozen claim. The 10,868 expanded representations are a representation count, **not** 10,868 independent facts or accuracy gain. **Status:** `HISTORY_REPORTED_UNVERIFIED / NO_GAIN`.

## Retrieval and answerability must remain separate scoreboards

A clean build must explicitly count, at minimum:

`identity resolution → document authority/admission → candidate retrieval@K (exact/lexical/dense/fused) → canonical/view collapse → owner/configuration/reranking → selected sufficient evidence@K → bounded expansion → serialized model-visible context sufficiency → answerability/abstention → realized answer correctness → claim-level citation/grounding validity`.

Each stage needs cases, denominator, valid/blocked/invalid classification, failure cause, source revision and run identity. The historical **100/100 exact-gold@10 versus 0/100 semantic reachability@10** in the 110-case development experiment (100-case metric subset), the **17-case no-gain paired contextualization**, and the November R2 **P5/P6 0/5** sufficient contexts illustrate why a single “RAG accuracy” number is misleading. A rank improvement that does not improve selected, applicable evidence or the answer is at best an intermediate diagnostic.

## Clean-build inheritance: decisions and falsifiable gates

**Inherit by default, with new implementation and regression tests:** source/document governance; exact model/part identifiers separate from semantics; typed owner and configuration authority; fact-specific DB vs manual authority; canonical evidence identity and provenance; lexical + dense retrieval with transparent fusion; bounded page/procedure/table parent-child expansion; context/source serialization; sufficiency with abstention/clarification; claim-to-citation binding; isolated immutable evaluation. These are *principles and stage contracts*, not an instruction to copy old runtime modules.

**Retest before adoption:** Docling extraction/accurate tables, OCR for raster and diagram evidence, local cross-encoder, TABLE relation resolver/detector, richer owner permission graphs, contextualized full views, multiview expansion, metadata narrowing and neural/G2 ordering. Every experiment must predeclare expected downstream endpoint, frozen comparable cases, source/index/model/prompt identity, representation budget, and safe failure thresholds. Demonstrate paired improvement in **correct owner + sufficient evidence + sufficient serialized context + realized cited answer** without contamination or latency regressions.

**Avoid by default:** treating file-path-derived aliases as source authority; expanding across procedures, owner products or conflicting configurations; propagating false positives from a table term match; unconditional full-view proliferation; treating an observational detector as an active gate; overstrict response suppression; ranking movement or token/index counts as success; using invalid experiments as qualified evidence; binding production/research/evaluation modules together based only on version labels.

**Suggested staged tests:**
1. Exact identity/document-owner admission: unseen model/revision/alias/variant negatives; distinguish `UNKNOWN` from `CONFLICT`.
2. Typed unit/relationship recall: table subject/column/unit and procedure ordered-step fidelity, including injected relationship corruption and OCR/diagram exceptions.
3. Retrieval-to-answer pipeline: same-query source-bound paired standard vs any proposed new mechanism, tracking per-stage candidate, evidence, context, answer, citation and abstention outcomes.
4. Cost/scale: compare 40/10-type outcomes on a *new frozen matched* corpus and unseen holdout, with full indexing/latency costs, not historical denominators reused as qualification.
5. Promotion: only with immutable run package, explicit failure accounting, independent scoring, authorized data and accepted safety thresholds.
