Do not reintroduce without a new hypothesis, holdout and downstream improvement. Scope limited to the tested mechanism.

- **REJECT implicit configuration authority.** Observe-only relationships must not silently become active evidence.
- **REJECT hardcoded manufacturer/example routing.** Product and source applicability remain generic.
- **REJECT treating 100% exact-gold@10 on bounded reconstructions as 100% end-to-end accuracy.**
These are evidence-informed architectural guardrails. Review experimental register before extending them beyond tested conditions.
